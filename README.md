### Research Highlights

- **Differentiable Hydrology** – building hybrid physics-ML models (MILC, GAN-PO) for distributed land-surface modelling.
- **Continental-scale Deep Learning** – rainfall–runoff modelling across India's diverse, gauged & ungauged basins.
- **Climate Extremes** – compound extremes, ETCCDI indices, CMIP6 streamflow projections over South Asia & the Himalayas.
- **Hazard Analytics** – cloudburst, landslide, and flood risk assessment with satellite + gauge data fusion.
