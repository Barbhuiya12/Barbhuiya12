## 🔬 &nbsp;Research Highlights

<table>
  <tr>
    <td width="50%" valign="top">

#### 💧 Differentiable Hydrology
Hybrid **physics-ML** models (MILC-δ, GAN-PO) embedding neural networks
inside differentiable physics frameworks for distributed land-surface
modelling.

#### 🌧 Continental-scale Deep Learning
Rainfall–runoff modelling across **144+ Indian basins** —
GRU, LSTM, EA-LSTM, AR-LSTM, Transformer, Informer, Reformer, Linformer,
plus state-space (Mamba) experiments.

</td>
    <td width="50%" valign="top">

#### 🌡 Climate Extremes & CMIP6
Compound extremes, ETCCDI indices, **CMIP6 streamflow projections**
across South Asia and the Himalayas; nonstationary FFA.

#### 🛰 Hazard Analytics
**Cloudburst, landslide, flood** risk with satellite + gauge fusion
(ERA5, IMD, SAR), WRF dynamics, and remote-sensing soil-moisture ML.

</td>
  </tr>
</table>
